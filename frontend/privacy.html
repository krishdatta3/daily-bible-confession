Privacy Policy

Daily Bible Confession · Last updated: 17 June 2026

Daily Bible Confession (“the App”, “we”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the App handles and why. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Handle

  • On-device data: your prayer journal notes, favourite verses, daily streaks, faith points, and your language, theme and reminder-time settings. This information is stored only on your device and is not uploaded to us.
  • AI feature text: when you use “Create My Confession”, the Verse Reflection, or the Faith Assistant chat, the text you enter (and the selected Bible verse) is sent over a secure connection to our server and to our AI providers to generate a response. The App does not require an account and this text is not linked to your identity. It is used only to produce your response.
  • Audio narration: when you tap “Listen”, the verse or prayer text is sent to a text-to-speech provider so audio can be generated for you.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or payment information. The App contains no advertisements and we do not sell your data.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

To provide the optional AI and audio features, the App uses:

  • Google (Gemini) — text generation for confessions, reflections and chat.
  • OpenAI (and ElevenLabs, if enabled) — text-to-speech audio.

These providers process the text you submit only to return a result. Please review their respective privacy policies for details.

3. Notifications

With your permission, the App shows local daily reminders scheduled on your device. You can turn these off at any time in the App’s Settings or in your device settings.

4. Children

The App is suitable for all ages and is not directed at collecting personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

On-device data is removed when you delete the App or clear its data. Because AI requests are not tied to an account, there is no personal profile stored on our side to delete. For any privacy request, contact us at the email below.

6. Security

All network requests use encrypted HTTPS connections.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
